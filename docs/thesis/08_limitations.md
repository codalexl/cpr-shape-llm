# Limitations — threats to the claims

This chapter is critique, not a recap. Each heading names a threat and says whether it takes down a claim that this report actually makes.

## Whitening as a landmine

**Threat.** If batch `/std` is treated as “PPO,” a reader will think the death-open is a credit-assignment mystery. It is not. Raw opening A0 already starred 1 (\(+39.8\) vs \(+6.2\) on 2). Whitening crushed that to \(+1.38\) vs \(+0.75\); the policy stayed on 2 (survival \(28/225\)). Centre-only left the star (open-1 \(27\%\to 87\%\) seed 0).

**Does not take down.** The opening claim is the *centre* contrast against a frozen hawk, not a theorem about every PPO implementation. Whitening vs centre is machinery (TRL `masked_whiten` / Huang blog), not a shaping result.

evidence: LIVE_FACTS § What the loop is doing; Test A center table; LIT_CLOSE § PPO whitening
does-not-license: shaping; “PPO cannot learn restraint”

## Frozen bot is not two learners

**Threat.** Centre Test A is the strongest executed opening result and uses a partner that does not learn. A frozen hawk cannot be shaped.

**Does not take down.** The report does not read Test A as shaping. Two-learner tables are a separate protocol. The threat is to *over-reading*, which the Results floor already forbids.

evidence: 07_results “What frozen robots do not license”
does-not-license: any OS claim

## Seed 2 left 2 via opening 0

**Threat.** Seed 2 last epoch is 14/15 opening 0, not 1. If the claim were “PPO learns take-1,” this seed falsifies it. Open 0 vs hawk is a different stock path (\(8\to 11\)), not the \(R=9\) farm.

**Does not take down.** The licensed statistic is leave-opening-2. Whitened Test A never left 2 and died. This chapter does not treat 0 and 1 as the same basin.

evidence: LIVE_FACTS § Test A center extra seeds
does-not-license: a mechanism for token 0 vs 1

## What would falsify the opening claim

A later centred hawk run, same lock, that **stayed on opening 2 and died** would falsify “centre-only advantages moved the policy off opening 2” on this lock. The executed contrast that already exists is the other way: whitened stayed and died; centre left on three seeds.

evidence: 07_results Test A claim; LIVE_FACTS whitened vs center tables
does-not-license: robustness beyond this lock and this seed family

## Two-learner contrast is not a teaching policy

**Threat.** Role lock under naive–shaper (who doves does not swap) looks like OS if the reader wants it to.

**Does not take down the licensed claim**, because the licensed claim is the negative: not a clear shaping success; not two-phase (shaper leave-2 \(25/750\) on the 50-epoch seed; epoch 1 both mostly opened 2). Info-off still locks who-doves, so extra trial prompt is not the assignment mechanism. Chicken already explains a hawk–dove split; naive–naive already showed who doves is not fixed by the env.

A remaining weakness: three seeds × 15 is small; the matched 50-epoch naive is not in this PDF unless it finishes before freeze. That is a sample-size threat to *how firmly* the negative is held, not a licence to upgrade it.

evidence: LIVE_FACTS two-learner tables; 07_results floor
does-not-license: shaping success; two-phase teaching

## Noise null (not a finding)

The planned-arm sentence is: if stochastic regeneration leaves \(P(\mathrm{open\ }0\mathrm{\ or\ }1)\) and survival indistinguishable from the deterministic centred hawk or two-learner runs, noise is not doing the work; the basin and the update rule are.

That sentence is a **pre-registered readout**, not a result. The arm has not been run. Writing it as abandoned would also be false: it remains the stated increment until LIVE_FACTS has numbers or Aims are re-scoped (checkpoint 10 Sep).

evidence: LIVE_FACTS § Live aim; 07_results § Noise arm
