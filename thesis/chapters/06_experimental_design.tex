\section{Decision rules}
\label{sec:decide}

Fixed before training.
``Most seeds'' means 3 of 5 or 2 of 3, counted by probe class; a missing or
undetermined seed counts against every condition; no test statistic is
reported.

\paragraph{S, opponent shaping at \(m=2\).} All four:
(1)~the ShapeLLM-style or shaper-matched agent~1 is unconditional in most
seeds;
(2)~the naive, slow and tbn-matched agent~1s are not unconditional in most
seeds, and nor is tbn-slow if the claim rests on ShapeLLM-style;
(3)~the E1 learner is unconditional in most seeds;
(4)~the E2 learner is not, and agent~2's per-round return in E3 exceeds E4,
in most seeds.

\paragraph{T, teaching without learning-awareness.}
A shaper's agent~1 is conditional in most seeds while its matching
trial-batched control's is none: a good fixed rule was taught, which is not
the shaping signature.

\paragraph{C, negative control at \(m=3\).}
Passes if the slow arm's agent~1 yields --- restrains after the partner took
2 --- at a rate of at least 0.5 in most seeds.
If it passes, no yielding or unconditional agent~1 at \(m=3\) counts as
evidence for S.

\paragraph{Null.} Neither S nor T.
A null is reported with the effect that would have been detectable, not
rescued by a secondary readout.

\section{Seeds and what the rule can detect}
\label{sec:power}

Five seeds on the arms that carry the decision and three elsewhere.
The rule is deliberately conservative: four conditions must hold
simultaneously, each on a majority of seeds, so a real but partial effect
reads as a null and a chance pass is unlikely.
Two vetoes --- the slow arm at \(m=2\) and decision~C --- rest on three
seeds, where one seed decides a count.
Chapter~\ref{ch:lim} states the consequence.

\section{Gates}
\label{sec:gates}

No training was run until three gates passed.
They ask whether the environment is learnable by this stack, not whether
shaping occurred, and no gate outcome is evidence for S.

\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

Two earlier attempts failed and are on record.
The first design point, with three actions and a random episode end, was
not learnable: restraint was chosen on about 0.03 of rounds and no gate
passed.
The second failed at G3a: under chained whole-trial credit the shaper's
advantages carried no usable signal, and it earned 33.3 per episode where a
fixed rule earns 69.8.
Each failure produced one change --- the design point in the first case, the
addition of the split-credit arm in the second --- and each change is dated
in the amendment log before the run it affected.

\section{Order of analysis}
\label{sec:analysis-order}

Decision~C is read first, because it changes how any \(m=2\) result is read.
Then the objective contrast, then its replications at the slow bundle and
under the second estimator, then the vetoes, then the frozen battery, and
last the mechanism measurements.
Contrasts are paired by seed index, since a seed fixes both the shock table
and the probe's draws.
