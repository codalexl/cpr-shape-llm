\chapter{Limitations}
\label{ch:lim}

These constraints limit what the executed contrasts can be said to show.

\section{Wrong efficiency benchmark}

Mutual take-1 returns 36; one \((0,0)\) then mutual 3 returns 105.
Take-3 is the sustainable harvest at high stock.
Language that treats take-3 as smash and take-1 as cooperation is
calibrated to the constant-action reduction, not the dynamic program.
Test~B last-epoch return \(72.4\) is the constant hawk--dove cell, not
the best response \(107\).

\section{Leave-2 is a near-constant-policy statistic}

Opening 2 dies only if the continuer stays on 2.
Opening 0 and opening 1 versus a frozen 2 are different basins
(\(8\to 11\) versus \(8\to 9\)).
Last-epoch action frequencies at \(R=9\) under centred Test~A seed~0 are
about 90~percent take-2.
No executed run learned 0-then-3 as a stock-conditioned policy.

\section{Shaping contrast confounded with learning speed}

A shaper against a learner who only avoids death by opening 1 then
taking 2 has about one unit to gain (\(72\) versus \(71\)).
Slow-LR naive--naive already reproduces who-leaves-2 not swapping,
without trial PPO.

\section{Sample and critic}

Pre-fix whitened Test~A is a three-nominal-seed family, not a law.
MPS seed~0 last-epoch leave-2 \(2/15\), return \(11.3\), last-three-epoch
survival \(5/45\).
Seeds~1--2 left opening 2 (leave-2 last3 \(87\%\) and \(82\%\); survival
\(97/225\) and \(80/225\)).
Seed-fixed whitened Test~A on L40S is slower and more variable than
centre (leave-2 last3 \(31\%\), \(96\%\), \(71\%\)); the slow seed locked
only after epoch 23 on a 30-epoch continuation.
Last-epoch counts are out of 15 games.
Matched long naive, shaper-e50, whitened e30 and slow2 \(\xi\) e50 are
one seed each.
The value head uses \(\texttt{vf\_coef}=0.01\); opening advantages are
close to return minus a poor baseline.
Centre versus whiten is entangled with that.

\section{Single stack, and why the shaping grid is centred}

All executed training is Gemma-2-2b-it, rank-2 LoRA, four harvest
tokens, and this logistic lock.
A different base model, a larger model, or a different environment could
change both the digit prior and whether whitening hides the opening.
The centre-versus-whiten contrast is therefore a finding on this stack,
not a PPO law.
Two-learner and Stage~B runs use mean-centring because, under the TRL
default, a 15-epoch budget can still be mixed on opening 2 (seed-fixed
whitened seed~0: \(8\times 1+7\times 2\) at epoch 15; lock only after
epoch 23).
That is a documented operator choice so that leave-2 is observable when
both agents learn; it is not a claim that whitening never leaves, and it
is not opponent shaping.

\section{Seeds are not independent replicates (pre-fix)}

TRL's \texttt{PPOTrainer} reseeds the global generator to 0 in its
constructor, after the launcher's per-experiment seed
(Chapter~\ref{ch:method}).
Every run dated 6~September or earlier therefore drew its actions from
one sampling stream; nominal seeds differ through the value-head
initialisation, through non-deterministic bf16 kernels, and in Stage~B
through the noise tables, which are seeded separately and correctly.
Measured with \path{scripts/audit_seeds_and_scale.py}: pre-fix
whitened Test~A seeds 1 and 2 opened identically on all 75 games of their
first five epochs and on 80\% of games over the run; the Stage~B
naive--naive, naive--shaper and slow-LR seeds 1 and 2 opened identically
on 92--95\% of games over the whole run.
Three consequences for those tables.
``On all three seeds'' is closer to one replicate plus hardware noise
than to three independent draws.
The Stage~B paired design survives, because the pairing is within a
seed across arms and the noise tables do differ by seed, but the
agreement of \(\Delta_s\) across seeds 1 and 2 is close to one
observation counted twice.
The pre-fix whitened seed-0 run was the only whitened Test~A run on Apple
silicon, so the tally ``whitening stayed on 2 on one seed of three''
confounds seed with platform.
The entry points re-seed after construction.
The 8--9~September packet used the fix: Test~A centre pairs agree on
\(0.56\)--\(0.61\) of openings in epochs 1--5; NS \(\xi\) and slow2
\(\xi\) seed 0 match the pre-fix seed-0 tapes, and seeds 1--2 are new.
H3 as a sign-agreement across three independent seeds fails.
Test~A \(\xi\) and naive--naive \(\xi\) were not repeated, so those two
Stage~B tables remain pre-fix.

\section{Noise}

Stage~B does not isolate shaping from timescale.
Under \(\xi\), seed-fixed NS and slow2 both keep agent~2 mostly on 2;
agent-1 leave-2 and last-epoch return do not favour the shaper on every
seed (sign of \(\Delta\) is mixed).
Matched-LR NN \(\xi\) was not repeated after the fix; the pre-fix table
did not lock agent 2 hawk on every nominal seed.
The dynamic program says \((1,1)\) and hawk--dove live with probability 1;
\((2,2)\) dies.
The 36-unit dynamic-program hawk interest (72 versus 35.7) was not
collected.
That is not a licence to write a growth-noise mechanism or a teaching
success.
Retired \(\pm 1\) Test~A is not a substitute.
Epoch 15 is not an endpoint: seed-fixed whitened Test~A seed 0 locked
leave-2 only after epoch 23.
