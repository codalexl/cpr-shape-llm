\chapter{Limitations}
\label{ch:lim}

Bodies in this chapter transcribe sentences already owned in
Chapter~\ref{ch:results} or locked as method facts.
They are not a new interpretation.

\section{Whitening as a landmine}

Raw opening advantage already starred the \(R=8\to 9\) basin against a
frozen hawk.
Under batch whitening the star was compressed until the prior won on
count: the rare jackpot is the batch standard deviation, so the star
shrinks itself, and many openings of 2 outvote few openings of 1.
Centre-only advantages left the opening star.
That contrast is machinery, not a shaping result.

\section{Frozen bot is not two learners}

A frozen hawk is not two learning agents.
Centre-only Test~A does not imply that a shaper works.

\section{Seed 2 as the same opening family}

Seed 2 of centred Test~A is not a failed replicate.
Openings 0 and 1 are the same opening family: take less than 2 at \(R=8\).
Which restraint token wins can move with the seed.
Whitened Test~A never left opening 2 and died.

\section{What would falsify the opening claim}

The executed contrast that already exists: whitened Test~A never left
opening 2 and died; centred Test~A left opening 2 on three seeds.
A later centred hawk run that stayed on opening 2 and died would
falsify ``unflattened GAE is enough for PPO to leave the death-open''
on this lock.

\section{Two-learner contrast is not a teaching policy}

It is not a clear shaping success.
This report does not claim that the shaper taught the naive to take 1
and then exploited by taking 2.
Extra trial prompt is not what assigns who doves (info-off: who-doves
still did not swap).
Chicken explains a hawk--dove split; naive--naive already showed who doves
is not fixed by the env alone.
Agent 2 leave-2 on the 50-epoch seed was \(25/750\); epoch 1 the shaper
opened 2 while the naive still mostly opened 2.

\section{Noise null}

The planned-arm sentence already written in Results is:
``If stochastic regeneration leaves \(P(\mathrm{open}\ 0\mathrm{\ or}\ 1)\)
and survival indistinguishable from the deterministic centred hawk or
two-learner runs, noise is not doing the work; the basin and the update
rule are.''
That sentence is not a finding.
The arm has not been run.

\section{The lock is a method fact}

\((R_0,K,T,\texttt{rate\_tenths})=(8,40,36,9)\) is a method lock, not a
literature calibration.
Reed (1979) is an aim-column citation for planned noise on this same locked
update, not a source of those four integers
(Chapter~\ref{ch:env}).
