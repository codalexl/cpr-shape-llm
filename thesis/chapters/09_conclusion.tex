\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{ch:conc}

\section{Task}

This report asked whether ShapeLLM-style trial PPO can be run on a
sequential common-pool resource that is chicken rather than a payoff
table, and whether a noise increment on that locked update is worth
trying.
The executed substrate is integer logistic CPR
(\(R_0=8\), \(K=40\), \(T=36\), \(\texttt{rate\_tenths}=9\)).
The noise arm has not been run; a null on that arm remains reportable.

\section{Method}

Against a frozen hawk, centre-only advantages left opening 2;
batch whitening did not.
The prior yields on the first action.
It does not become a habit of take-1 across the tape.
Against a frozen dove the learner does not copy take-1.

\section{What two learners did not show}

Two centred learners split hawk--dove: who doves swapped under
naive--naive and did not under the shaper configs (info-on and info-off).
That is a role lock, not a clear shaping success, and not a two-phase
teaching policy (shaper leave-2 on the 50-epoch seed: \(25/750\)).
