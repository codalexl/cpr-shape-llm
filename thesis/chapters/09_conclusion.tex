\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{ch:conc}

\section{RQ1 --- structure}

A sequential integer CPR can be specified so that a linear-regrowth grid
is a dead learning problem against a take-2 opponent, and a logistic lock
has an interior dilemma that a peaked digit prior actually hits.
The live game is chicken: \((1,1)\) lives, \((2,2)\) dies around round~4,
hawk versus dove scores 72.
Mutual take-1 is not the joint optimum (\(W^{*}=210\) in Stage~A).
Leave-2 is a statistic for a near-constant prior class; opening 0 and
opening 1 versus a hawk are different basins.

\section{RQ2 --- advantage normalisation}

Against a frozen hawk, seed-fixed centre-only advantages left opening 2
on three independent L40S seeds by epoch 8.
Whitening left on a longer and more variable schedule (majority at
epochs 15, 7, 13); the slow seed locked only after epoch 23 on a
30-epoch continuation.
Do not write that whitening always keeps the death-open.
They differ by \(1/\sigma_{\mathcal{B}}\), measured per update at
\(13\)--\(15\); under Adam that is a reweighting of the policy gradient
against the critic, the entropy bonus and the other batches, not a
larger step \eqref{eq:logit-update}.
In the pre-fix MPS run that stayed on opening 2 the post-operator
advantage of opening 2 never turned negative, because no batch contained
a surviving game to compare against.
The prior yields on the first action; it does not become a tape-wide
take-1 habit.
Against a frozen dove the learner does not copy take-1.
The finding is a precondition for opponent shaping on this stack, not a
shaping result: two-learner runs use mean-centring so that leave-2 is
visible inside 15 epochs.
It is not claimed for other models or environments.

\section{RQ3 --- opponent shaping}

Two centred learners split hawk--dove.
Who doves swapped under matched-LR naive--naive and did not under the
shaper (info-on and info-off) or under a slow-LR naive--naive control
that matches the shaper's learning rate and clip.
On one 50-epoch seed the matched naive pair opened \((1,1)\) and agent 2
earned \(98.1\), in the direction of the \((1,1)\)-then-\((2,3)\) cell;
the matched shaper pair earned about \(143\) joint against that pair's
\(167\).
One seed each.

Under Stage~B \(\xi\), pre-fix Test~A seeds 0 and 2 opened 0 (the
\(0.80\)-survival exit versus a hawk) and did not learn stock-conditioned
feedback; that arm was not repeated after the seed fix.
Paired on the same noise tables after the fix, naive--shaper versus
slow2 is mixed: agent-1 leave-2 last3 \(96/44/33\%\) versus
\(69/76/27\%\), last-epoch return \(49.7/29.0/12.3\) versus
\(44.8/52.9/13.0\); both agent 2s stay mostly on 2.
Learning rate and clip are matched, trial update and prompt are not.
The 36-unit DP interest in feedback restraint was not collected.
H3 as a shaper-return and more-reliable-dove inequality fails at three
independent seeds.
