\section{Structure}

The two-player integer logistic CPR is the mean-field reduction of
Commons Harvest, solvable exactly, and under constant strategies it is a
chicken game: mutual restraint lives, mutual take-2 dies, and the
asymmetric hawk--dove profiles are the strict equilibria.
Its exact solution answers the first question before any training run.
In the deterministic game a naive learner already leaves the collapsing
opening against a hawk, the continuation after a safe opening is a
constant action, and a committed hawk gains at most one unit from
anything a shaper could induce; a shaping experiment there is unpowered
by construction.
Under a mean-one multiplicative shock on the growth increment, chosen so
that the constant-strategy cells are invariant, the continuation becomes
a stock-conditioned decision, the hawk's stake in its partner's feedback
rule is 36 units and its ceiling against a partner it has taught to
restrain is 105.5, and the joint optimum and every safe policy are
constant-escapement rules in Reed's sense.
That is why the thesis moves from Stage~A to Stage~B, and it is a result
of the dynamic programme, not an assumption.

\section{What was learned about the method}

Three findings about the training loop stand independently of the grid.
A library reseed in the PPO trainer silently replaced the experiment's
seed, so that replicate counts in earlier drafts overstated the evidence;
the audit script that found it is part of the repository.
The reward carries an adaptive KL penalty to the reference model that
earlier drafts did not name and that defends the digit prior the learner
starts from.
Under Adam the difference between whitened and centred advantages is not
a step size but a reweighting of the policy gradient against the critic,
the entropy bonus and the other batches, and the run that stayed on the
collapsing opening is the one whose batches never contained a survivor to
compare against.

Code, records, noise tables and every script that produced a number in
this report: \url{https://github.com/codalexl/cpr-shape-llm}.
